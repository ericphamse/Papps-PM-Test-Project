# 1.  Background

Kestrel operates metering, billing and network telemetry across four regional networks. Reporting is currently produced from overnight extracts into a legacy on-premises warehouse, and the business cannot answer questions about consumption or network loss without a two-day turnaround. Kestrel is building a cloud data platform to replace it. The target architecture is agreed and the ingestion layer for two of the four networks is in production. Kestrel is seeking one Senior Data Engineer at Level 4 to work within an existing data team of six.

# 2.  Scope of services

The successful candidate will:

- Design, build and operate batch and streaming data pipelines feeding the cloud data platform.

- Migrate reporting workloads off the legacy on-premises warehouse with no interruption to statutory reporting obligations.

- Model data for analytical consumption, including dimensional modelling and the semantic layer used by business reporting tools.

- Own pipelines end to end, including scheduling, data quality monitoring, lineage and incident response.

- Implement and maintain data quality controls, validation rules and reconciliation between source systems and the platform.

- Improve platform delivery practices, including infrastructure as code, environment management and automated testing of data transformations.

- Provide technical leadership: code review, design input, and mentoring of junior data engineers and analysts.

- Communicate data limitations, assumptions and modelling decisions to non-technical stakeholders, including regulatory reporting staff.
